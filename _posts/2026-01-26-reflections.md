## explanation from the writer

this song is about silent moments where two people are aware of each other without speaking. it is not about criticism or judgment in the usual sense. it is about observation, awareness, and recognition.

when i say judging you judging me, i mean the quiet evaluation that happens through eye contact, pauses, and body language. both people are reading each other at the same time. no one is in control and no one is innocent. there is no referee because both sides are equally involved.

the mirror and glass imagery represents distance that still allows clarity. there is a barrier, but it is transparent. you can see the other person clearly, and in doing so, you start seeing yourself just as clearly. the tension in the song comes from that realization.

what i want to convey is that many times we think we are reacting to others, but we are actually confronting parts of ourselves. attraction, curiosity, and discomfort often reveal more about us than about the person we are looking at.

the song does not try to resolve this tension with a confession or a conclusion. instead, it accepts it. the trial ends not because someone wins, but because both sides recognize the truth. even if the mirror breaks, the understanding remains.

this song is about clarity without explanation, connection without pursuit, and self recognition through someone else’s eyes.
