> [AuthCentral](https://authcentral-prod.vercel.app/docs) : dedicated authentication and token lifecycle control plane for internal and external APIs. It is designed to be independent of external B2B identity providers like Ping or Okta, while still being robust enough to act as the internal “auth backbone” for your ecosystem. This service sits beside your API gateway, acting as the authoritative source for token validation and policy enforcement. It decouples your internal architecture from specific identity vendors and provides a single, consistent security contract for all your developers.
